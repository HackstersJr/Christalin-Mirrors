Key Highlight: The self-hosted Baileys WhatsApp gateway avoids Meta's official Business API and third-party platform fees (Interakt / Wati / Twilio typically run ₹3,000–₹25,000/month), saving an estimated ₹36,000–₹3,00,000 per year compared to a paid WhatsApp Business API plan of similar functionality.
Risk Note: Baileys connects via WhatsApp's unofficial Web protocol rather than Meta's sanctioned Business API. This is why it is free, but it also means the linked number is not covered by WhatsApp's official terms of service and carries a real (if generally low, for moderate send volumes) risk of being flagged or temporarily banned. For higher message volume or if this becomes business-critical, migrating to the official WhatsApp Business API is the safer long-term path — at the recurring cost shown above.
