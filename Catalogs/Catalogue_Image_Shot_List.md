# Catalogue image shot list

**Total: 103 images** (50 women's, 53 men's).

Photos for the `/catalogue` carousel. Until a photo exists for a service, the page shows a generated cream-and-blush card with an icon, so you can add images gradually.

## How to add them

1. Name each file exactly as in the **File name** column (lower case), e.g. `women-signature-facial.jpg`.
2. Put the files in `Frontend/src/assets/catalogue/`. Nothing else to change: the page picks them up automatically.
3. Accepted formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`.

## Specs for every image

- **Shape:** portrait 4:5, 1200 × 1500 px.
- **Size:** under about 300 KB each (export as JPG or WebP at quality 80).
- **Framing:** keep the subject in the middle 80%. The card has rounded corners and the zoomed view crops slightly.
- **Look:** natural or soft window light, warm and clean, cream / blush / sand tones, matching the brand book. No text, logos, prices or watermarks on the image.
- **People:** model or client release needed for any face. Hands, feet and hair-only shots avoid this.
- **Shared images:** body-area services (Bleach, D-Tan, Waxing) use one image per collection, so they need far fewer photos.
